---
name: Portfolio content boundaries
description: Standing scope and content constraints for the personal portfolio alongside EWS
---

Keep personal projects and experiments distinct from studio services and client work. Portfolio copy must be grounded in first-party public sources; do not infer employment, education, availability, credentials or results.

**Why:** The requested portfolio is an extension of the existing studio website, not a fabricated resume or a rebranding of personal experiments as client projects.

**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction when adding portfolio content or studio case studies. Store research citations in content documentation, not memory. Keep authentication, admin permissions, inquiry persistence and prices outside portfolio-content changes unless explicitly requested.
